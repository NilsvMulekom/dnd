Session 3 Combat steps included

The party starts in The Sunken Sanctum.

The floor is flooded with knee high water. Each member of the party is standing on a stone pillar. Which triggered the Vale shard glow. After which a Drowned Warden and a Drowned echo come out of the water. The Drowned Warden has purple light emanating from its chest. The water starts bubbling. The Drowned echo points at Bastion Ember. After which it threatens Elroy Stonehand who cries out in terror.

Combat starts

Varragoth Sarulf shoots a fire bolt at the Drowned echo and misses.

Lyra Moonwhisper shoots Magic missile at the Drowned Warden. Which groaned but does not look very affected.

Elroy Stonehand Yells he has an idea and throws Mr Tuttles at the Drowned Warden. It sinks beneath the water.

Bastion Ember jumps from the pillar and runs at the Drowned echo. The water does not seem to harm him but it does slowly rise. He swings at the Drowned echo slices off its right arm and saps it. Nero Au Aurelius IV shouts “Lekkers bezig pik”

Ren Ashvane shoot his bow at the Drowned echo and misses. He drops his backpack on the Pillar and steps off.

The Drowned echo looks at Bastion Ember, it is pissed off at Bastion Ember for cutting off its arm. It raises its other arm to attack but the water is too much so It has the lower the arm again.

Nero Au Aurelius IV shoots an Eldritch Blast at the Drowned Warden it misses and angers the Drowned Warden which comes toward Nero Au Aurelius IV. The Drowned Warden yells “It must remain, raises its arms and splashes them down. Two more Drowned echo’s spawn.

The water where Mr Tuttles hit the water starts too boil.

Varragoth Sarulf dashes at the Vale shard and jumps on the pillar it is resting on.

Elroy Stonehand steps off his pillar while crying that he want to look for Mr Tuttles. The water rises again.

Lyra Moonwhisper shoots a fire bolt and misses. Drops her backpack on her Pillar and steps off.

Io Starbloom drops her backpack and steps off her pillar. She casts a thorn whip at the Drowned echo. It severely weakens it. She also casts Entangle on the Drowned Warden, it resists it.

Bastion Ember dashes towards the Drowned Warden

Ren Ashvane shoots an arrow at the Drowned echo hitting it in the face and killing it.

Nero Au Aurelius IV leaves his backpack on the pillar and steps into the water.

The Drowned Warden roars loudly. Varragoth Sarulf is too slow to cover his ears and is Frightened

The coffin of Nero Au Aurelius I comes crashing down the stairs and lands in the water. Here this shall help you Crist Voss shouts down the stairs.

Varragoth Sarulf shoves the Vale shard into his backpack and runs for the exit.

Elroy Stonehand swims closer to the Drowned Warden

Lyra Moonwhisper tries to shoot another Fire bolt at the Drowned echo but fails to even cast the spell.

Io Starbloom shoots a spell but misses. She tries to climb back on here pillar but falls off again.

Bastion Ember swings his sword at the Drowned Warden while casting smite and does critical damage and yells “This is my town you are not welcome here. The Drowned Warden is badly injured but still stays up right.

Ren Ashvane shoots his bow at the Drowned Warden, throws his bow on his back and slashes it with his dagger.

The Drowned echo’s feel the Drowned Warden is weakened and come to its aid.

Nero Au Aurelius IV wades through the water towards the coffin of Nero Au Aurelius I and tries to climb in while Varragoth Sarulf holds the coffin steady. He does not manage to climb in and is now awkwardly hanging off the side.

The Drowned Warden tries to hit Bastion Ember and Ren Ashvane with both arms hitting them both.

Varragoth Sarulf shoves the coffin towards Nero Au Aurelius IV, takes his hammer out and swing it a Drowned echo taking off its head and both arms.

Elroy Stonehand cries out that he wants to go home and he swims towards the exit.

Lyra Moonwhisper shoots a Fire bolt at the Drowned Warden

The Drowned Warden shouts, “You don’t know what’s going to happen”

Io Starbloom casts thorn whip but misses.

Bastion Ember Intimidates the Drowned Warden and yells “Tell us what you are trying to warn us from” Ren Ashvane adds “Or else!”

The Drowned Warden shouts “I was trying to protect it from him”

He shouts again and frightens Ren Ashvane. Bastion Ember is so unphased that the Drowned Warden steps back. Bastion Ember takes the opportunity to hit the Drowned Warden. The Drowned Warden says “You should have listened”.

Varragoth Sarulf dashes towards the exit.

Elroy Stonehand shouts “don’t leave us”, the water starts to rise more quickly. As he swims towards the door.

Varragoth Sarulf runs out the tomb and encounters a shadowy cat.

Lyra Moonwhisper shoots a fire bolt at the Drowned Warden and does critical damage. The Drowned Warden seems to sink down a bit, and no longer seems very responsive.

Io Starbloom makes a dash for Mr Tuttles

Bastion Ember shouts “We have almost got you, tell us who you mean”. Ren Ashvane you should ask him to explain it using S.T.A.R method (for which he gets heroic inspiration). The Drowned Warden says “He wants to take it again, he’s no good. He wants to take it again Vorath kaine”

Ren Ashvane shouts very scared “We don’t have to fight if you very specifically describe what the person you mean looks like”

The Drowned Warden points at the coffin “Him again”

Nero Au Aurelius IV partially climbs into the coffin. hanging off the side, the coffin drifts towards Elroy Stonehand. He tries to fully climb in, but fully falls out again.

The Drowned Warden roars again and summons 5 Drowned echo’s at the far end of the room.

Varragoth Sarulf is standing outside the room. The cat is looking at him and says “Meow”

Elroy Stonehand is hanging off the side of the coffin and yells “I am getting very tired”

Io Starbloom starts running towards the exit and picks up one of the backpacks from the pillar.

Bastion Ember swings his sword and smites the Drowned Warden. He yells “Karma is a bitch” as he slices the Drowned Warden in half. The Drowned Warden disappears in a cloud of purple smoke,

All Drowned echo’s seem to merge back into the walls.

Elroy Stonehand is still very worries about Mr Tuttles. He runs towards the middle of the room and shouts “MR TUTTLES!”. Mr Tuttles flies towards Elroy Stonehand and the room starts to shake. Elroy Stonehand faints after expending all of his energy. His eyes light up so brightly everybody is blinded for a few seconds.

Bastion Ember runs towards Elroy Stonehand picks him up and casts Lay on Hands. Elroy Stonehand asks “where am I”.

Varragoth Sarulf sees that when Elroy Stonehand shouts that the eyes of the cat light up.

End of combat

Ren Ashvane goes outside and uses the Shadow fragment to inspect his surrounding. On the walls he sees shadows of people. He notices he sees the same amount of people as the Drowned echo’s in the crypt. Nero Au Aurelius IV attempts to see if he recognizes any of the shadows. He feels like the Drowned echo’s used to be people.

Bastion Ember asks why Crist Voss mentioned he was not allowed to leave where he was. Crist Voss dodges the question by saying he heard trouble and wanted to help.

The party follows Crist Voss to his home. When the party goes to sleep, Ren Ashvane drinks some coffee and keeps watch.

The party levels up.
